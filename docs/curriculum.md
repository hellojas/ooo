## PDF library → practice blocks
Drive folder: https://drive.google.com/drive/u/0/folders/1elPTItMEB3brrxqwe8mt9ja8pev32DEr
Subfolders: standards lab · arrangement · soloing technique · openstudiojazz ·
reference · sax · archive · harrylikas.

- **Technique / voicings (45 min):** Open Studio *LHV workbook* (wk 1–3),
  *JCB – Piano* comping (wk 4–6); Andy LaVerne *Tons of Runs*, Mike Gorman
  *drop2* + *Upper Structure Polychords* (wk 7–10). *Barry Harris Method* PDF
  as reference for Sundays/Kaufman Tue, not a standalone method.
- **Standard of the week (75 min):** lead sheets from Real Book collection /
  catbus fake book. Autumn Leaves mini-course already in `standards lab`
  (lead sheet → 1+1 → fill examples → Gorman chord-changes improv) is the
  template for every tune. Gorman *Targetting 3rds* and *Progressive
  vocabulary & phrase building* = chord-tone soloing drills on the week's tune.
- **Ear / transcription (60 min):** no book on purpose. Aebersold handbook and
  Reeves *Creative Jazz Improvisation* are look-up references. Gorman's
  analysed charts + MIDI (Embraceable You, Polkadots, For All We Know, Danny
  Boy, In Love In Vain, Stella) are answer keys: transcribe first, then check.
- **Arrangement (30 min):** Gorman *Harmonising a melody*, *Skylark top-note
  function*. *1000 Arrangements* is a browse-for-ideas file: steal one
  arrangement per week that uses the LH pattern being built.
- **Bebop (wk 7–10):** David Baker *How to Play Bebop* vol. 1, *The Blues*.
- **Sax:** *improvisation_2_handbook (Eb)*, alto blues play-along. Ignore
  until week 4; then the week's standard head.
- Archive: *Jazz Piano for Beginners Workbook*, *intro to jazz*, *FQBK
  handbook*, Baker *Jazz Pedagogy*.
