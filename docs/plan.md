## Piano
**Ear training reframed.** EarMaster / Functional Ear Trainer drill isolated
intervals; playing a song by ear is hearing bass motion and function in a mix.
Apps capped at 10 min/day; the real work is transcription at the keyboard:
1. Find the key (sing tonic, check). Bass line first — loop 4 bars, sing, play.
2. Label every chord by function (I, vi, IV, V, ii, V/V, ♭VII…), out loud.
3. Add quality/extensions.
4. Play in original key, then two more keys.
5. Keep a running list of progressions found.

**Standards rotation, one per week:** Autumn Leaves, Blue Bossa, F Blues
(Billie's Bounce), All the Things You Are, Fly Me to the Moon, There Will
Never Be Another You, Bye Bye Blackbird, Someday My Prince Will Come, Take the
A Train, Summertime.

**Each week's standard:** days 1–2 melody + shell voicings, memorize the form;
days 3–4 rootless A/B voicings, walk chord tones 1-3-5-7 at tempo; day 5
chord-tone solo → add enclosures, record; weekend: head + chorus + comp, 5 min.

**Technique block:** wk 1–3 shells + rootless through 12 keys on ii–V–I;
wk 4–6 drop-2, bebop scales, enclosures; wk 7–10 quartal, upper structures,
one transcribed blues chorus (Bud Powell / Wynton Kelly), 2–3 Parker blues heads.

**Arrangement block:** yesterday's transcribed pop song → LH pattern (one per
week: root-5-octave, broken 1-5-10, syncopated bass + chord on the "and") →
RH melody with chord tones below. The LH must run on autopilot. One finished
arrangement per week, recorded.

## Gym
- Climbing: Mon/Wed/Fri Vital BK = floor (3×). Thu Vital LES = optional 4th. Open-hand only, no crimps, no
  hangboard while the R-hand PIP is inflamed; get it assessed week 1.
  Wk 1–3 volume/rebuild; wk 4–7 project purples (V6–7) on finger-friendly
  styles; wk 8–10 V8 attempts. Flare → drop a phase.
- Pull-ups: 5×5 weighted after runs (Tue, Sun) and Friday's climb; +2.5–5 lb
  a week; max test Sundays of weeks 3, 6, 10. Under 10 → grease the groove.
- Running: Tue fixed, Sun optional. C25K prescriptions per week in
  `data/training-phases.json`. Goal: 25–30 min continuous by week 10.
- Badminton: Kotofit LIC beginner open play, every other Saturday 10am.

## Sax
15–30 min daily at 9:30. Wk 1–3 long tones (tuner, chromatic from low B♭),
overtones, one simple melody. Wk 4+ add the week's standard head.

## Reading
3–4 books total, phone in the bag, rotate shops (see Coffee tab).

## Sunday review (30 min)
Record: the week's standard (head + one chorus), the by-ear arrangement,
pull-up/climb log. Listen back, note one fix, set next song.

## Milestones
- Week 3: 3 standards memorized w/ rootless voicings; 8+ pop songs transcribed
  and functionally labeled; 15 consecutive sax days; finger assessed.
- Week 6: 6 standards; chord-tone solo at tempo on any; 3 finished
  arrangements; back on purples; 30-min run.
- Week 10: 10 standards; new top-40 song → chords + playable arrangement in
  30 min; first V8 attempts; 10+ pull-ups or +20 lb × 5; 40-min run (or C25K
  wk 7–8 at two runs a week).
